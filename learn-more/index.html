Free, trusted places to keep exploring the brain.

  • PhET Neuron simulation

    From the University of Colorado Boulder. Zap a single neuron and watch how it fires: ion channels open, charged particles move, and a signal travels along the cell. It runs in the browser and works on Chromebooks.

  • Neuroscience for Kids

    Created by neuroscientist Eric Chudler. Kid-friendly pages, experiments, and games about brain anatomy, the senses, and much more.

  • BrainFacts.org

    From the Society for Neuroscience. Articles, videos, and answers from scientists about how the brain and nervous system work.
