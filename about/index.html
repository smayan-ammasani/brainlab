About

I'm Smayan Ammasani, a student at Weddington High School (Class of 2028) studying computational neuroscience. I built this site because most neuroscience resources for middle school are text to read, and I wanted students to measure their own nervous systems instead. My own research looks at how expertise shows up in the timing of decisions, like how long chess players think before a move. The site is free for anyone to use.

Sources

Every science fact on this site comes from a published study or a trusted source, such as the Society for Neuroscience. The experiments are classroom versions of well-known research methods, adapted to run in a web browser. They are for learning, not for testing anyone.
