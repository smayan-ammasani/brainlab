Privacy

Short version: nothing you do here leaves your device.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts or logins.
  • No cookies.
  • No browser storage. The site doesn't save anything on your device, not even your settings.
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind.
  • No sending of results anywhere. Your numbers live in the open browser tab and disappear when you close it or reload the page.
  • Nothing loaded from other websites: no outside fonts, scripts, images, or videos.
  • No requests for your microphone, camera, location, or notifications.

How we know

Every page carries a security rule, called a Content Security Policy, that your browser enforces. One part of it, connect-src 'none', tells the browser to block the page from sending or fetching any data over the network. Other parts block anything from loading from other websites. So even if a mistake slipped into the code, your browser would stop it from sending your information anywhere.

The "Copy my results" button puts a line of text on your device's clipboard, and only when you press it. What happens to that text next is up to you.

What we can't control

  • The web host. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security reasons, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. See GitHub's page about GitHub Pages and the GitHub Privacy Statement linked from it.
  • Other websites. Links to other sites, like PhET or BrainFacts.org, follow those sites' own privacy policies. This site tells your browser not to share which page you came from when you follow a link.
  • Your school or device. Schools and device owners may keep their own records of which websites are visited.
